# ESO skill icons: source and fan-use assessment

Checked 2026-10-27 UTC. Scope: low-resolution in-game ability icons used next to explanatory skill reference entries on a noncommercial, unofficial fan site, with local GitHub-hosted assets. Not a general asset library, commercial license, or ownership claim.

## Rights basis

- [Official ESO staff guidance, Kai Schober, May 2019](https://forums.elderscrollsonline.com/de/discussion/472433/erlaubnis-fuer-screenshot-gallerie): the Senior Community Manager says screenshots and other game assets can in principle be used for noncommercial purposes, including screenshot galleries. He expressly does not grant a blank check and requires compliance with conduct rules. The current public page was read successfully on the check date.
- [Official ESO staff guidance, Jessica Folsom, April 2015](https://forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/167935/who-owns-copyright-on-screenshots-and-images-taken-in-game-is-there-and-where-is-a-zos-policy): a staff answer described as reviewed by the legal team permits fan creations subject to the service rules, respectful use, copyright/trademark notices, and no commercial or monetary-gain use. The current public page was read successfully on the check date.
- These are conditional game-owner fan-use statements, not Creative Commons/public-domain licenses. No claim is made that a third-party site's open-source code license licenses ZeniMax's game artwork. Bethesda's video policy is not used as permission for static icons.

## Acquisition source

- [Hyperioxes](https://hyperioxes.com/) publishes in-game icons on `static.hyperioxes.com/images/icons/` within its build references. Only observed URLs mapped to relevant game skills are eligible. Do not acquire its custom UI icons, illustrations, screenshots, editorial writing, or design assets.
- [Hyperioxes privacy policy](https://hyperioxes.com/privacy-policy) and visible site navigation/footer showed no additional extraction or reuse prohibition. This absence is not itself a license. `https://hyperioxes.com/robots.txt`, read directly with HTTP 200, permits crawling for `User-agent: *`; robots permission concerns acquisition, not copyright.
- [ESO Decoded terms](https://esodecoded.com/terms) welcome attributed reference use and AI crawling, prohibit service-degrading scraping and wholesale republication, and explicitly reserve the underlying game IP to its owner. Its disclaimer contains no extra icon restriction. It was considered, but the inspected skill index and Dark Magic line had no image elements; it is not presently the image source.
- ESO-Hub is excluded because of its collection restrictions. UESP's blocked page is not bypassed. No one was contacted and no assets were taken from behind login, paywall, or access control.

## Conditions for publication

1. Keep use noncommercial and contextual: one small icon beside the matching skill information, rather than a downloadable asset pack.
2. Include a visible notice: `非公式・非営利ファンサイト。ESOのゲーム画像・アイコンは ZeniMax Media Inc. / Bethesda Softworks / ZeniMax Online Studios に帰属します。権利元による提携・公認を示すものではありません。`
3. Credit the acquisition source and link to the relevant Hyperioxes source page; preserve per-file URL, source mapping, retrieval time, size, SHA-256, and game skill ID/name.
4. Do not put these game images under the repository's own code/content license. Mark them as third-party game assets under conditional noncommercial fan-use guidance.
5. Stop on a source restriction, access denial, uncertain skill/image mapping, or changed rights conditions. Do not infer permission for advertising, sale, trademark branding, or unrelated artwork.

## Limitations

This is a practical source review, not a legal opinion or a guarantee. The official staff guidance is old but remains publicly available. Requests to the newer Bethesda legal-document host returned HTTP 403; that access control was not bypassed. No newer specific skill-icon permission or prohibition was established. Attribution and low resolution do not independently create copyright permission.
